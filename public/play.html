WELCOME TO THE LLAMA LOUNGE!
HOW TO PLAY
  • Tap the floor to walk. Tap stools, cushions and loveseats to sit.
  • Type in the chat box to talk. Your words float above your llama.
  • Tap things to use them: the arcade, jukebox, lamp, bar cups, tables and shirt frames. Tap the rope to work the bar.
  • Tap another llama to wave or spit. Use MENU for emotes and more.
  • The pink button does the action for wherever you are. On the dance floor it does a FLIP (or press F).
  • Keep your llama fed and watered! The 🍔 and 💧 meters at the top go down while you play. Order food and drinks at the bar (sit on a bar stool and press ORDER), buy cider and snacks in LLAMA ACRES, or eat apples off the ground in LLAMA ACRES (+15 🍔). If a meter runs empty your llama walks slower and games pay half.
  • Earn LLAMA TOKENS by hitting arcade milestones and working the bar. Spend them on drinks (1 token), or bet up to 10 against THE HOUSE at the table games. Table games can also be played for free.
  • Five rooms: the LLAMA LOUNGE, LLAMA ACRES outside, the LLAMA CASINO, the SPIT ARENA and LLAMA LANES. Tap a room in the bar at the bottom to walk there. Harvest the carrot patch in LLAMA ACRES for tokens.
  • Try the DUNK TANK in LLAMA ACRES: stand on the grass in front of the target (left of the barn) and press THROW. Hit the bullseye to dunk whoever is on the seat (1 coin a throw, a dunk pays 3). Brave llamas can sit on the tank and earn a coin every time they get dunked.
  • In the SPIT ARENA, press PLAY for a 60-second spit-ball round. Play solo against rival llamas, or with everyone who joins. Earn tokens, level up to unlock spit colors, and keep a daily streak.
  • At LLAMA LANES, press BOWL for a 5-frame game of hay bale bowling. Swipe up to roll. Strikes, spares and high scores win coins.
  • Scroll, pinch, or use + and − to zoom.
LOUNGE RULES
  • Chat is not filtered. Swearing and crude jokes are allowed, and you may see offensive language.
  • Jokes are fine. Harassing a player is not: no targeting, bullying or threatening other llamas. BLOCK or REPORT anyone who does.
  • No spamming the chat.
  • No sharing personal info, yours or anyone else's.
  • No sexual or graphic content.
  • Spitting is for fun. Don't target someone who asks you to stop.
Llamas who break the rules may be removed from the lounge.
