1. About these terms

Llama Lounge ("the game", "we", "us") is an online hangout where players control llamas, chat, play mini-games, decorate rooms and collect items. By creating an account or playing, you agree to these Terms and the Privacy section below. If you don't agree, please don't play.

We may update these Terms as the game grows. If we make a big change, we'll let players know in the game. Playing after a change means you accept the new version.

2. Who can play

  • You must be 13 years old or older to make an account. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 13, we'll close it and delete its information.
  • If you're under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian is okay with you playing and has read these Terms.
  • You can't play if we've banned you before, or if the law where you live doesn't allow it.

3. Your account

  • Keep your password private. You're responsible for everything done on your account.
  • Use a real email you can get to. It's the only way to reset your password if you forget it.
  • Don't share, sell or trade accounts, and don't use someone else's.
  • Llama names and room names can't be rude, hateful or pretend to be staff. We may change or remove names that break the rules.

4. Community rules

Llama Lounge is satire from the La Llama Politically Incorrect Club. It makes fun of everything and everyone, and none of it is a real opinion or advice. Chat is not filtered. Swearing and crude jokes are allowed, and you may see language you find offensive. If that bothers you, this is not the game for you. These rules still apply to every player:

  • Don't harass players. Joking around is fine. Targeting, bullying, threatening or ganging up on another player is not, for any reason, including their race, religion, sex, sexuality, disability or background. If someone asks you to stop, stop.
  • No sexual content. No sexual talk or pictures, and nothing sexual involving or aimed at anyone under 18, ever.
  • Stay safe. Never share your real address, phone number, passwords or other private details, and don't ask others for theirs. Don't try to meet up with people you only know from the game.
  • No scams or spam. Don't trick people out of tokens or items, flood the chat, or advertise.
  • Play fair. No cheating, bots, hacking, or using bugs to get tokens or items. If you find a bug, report it with MENU > REPORT A BUG.
  • Don't pretend to be staff.

Nothing in chat or private messages is blocked or starred out. The game still notices swear words and slurs: if you send a lot of them in a short time you get a heads-up that your messages may offend some people, and staff can see that it happened. Llama names and room names are still checked and can't be rude or hateful. If a player is harassing you, use BLOCK or REPORT on their card. Staff can read the chat log and can mute, kick or ban.

5. Staff and moderation

The owner and moderators ("staff") keep the game safe. Staff can warn, mute, kick or ban players, reset room names, move furniture in player rooms, and look at chat logs. Staff actions are recorded. If you break these Terms, we may take any of these steps, remove items or tokens that were gained unfairly, or close the account. We try to be fair, but the final decision is ours.

  • TOKENS, clothes, furniture, wallpaper, floors, room kits and LIMITED RARES are all just part of the game. They have no real-money value, can't be cashed out, and aren't your property. You get a personal permission to use them in the game.
  • Tokens can't be bought with real money right now. If that ever changes, we'll add the rules for it here first.
  • Never buy or sell tokens, items or accounts for real money or for things outside the game. Accounts that do may be banned.
  • We may change prices, items, how much things fill or pay, and how the game's economy works. Items may be lost if an account is banned or closed, or if the game shuts down.

7. Trading and the marketplace

  • Trades between players and marketplace sales are final once they go through. Check every offer carefully before you press READY or BUY.
  • The marketplace keeps 5% of each sale. We may change this fee.
  • We don't undo trades or sales, except to fix a bug or stop a scam or cheating, and that's our call.

8. Player rooms and what you post

You're responsible for what you say and build in the game, including chat, messages, room names and how you decorate your room. Don't post anything you don't have the right to share. By posting in the game, you let us show, store and copy it as needed to run the game. We may remove anything that breaks these Terms.

9. Privacy

What we collect

  • Account details: your llama name, your email address, and your password. Passwords are stored only as a strong one-way scramble (PBKDF2), never as the password itself.
  • Game data: your tokens, items, clothes, scores, quests, friends, player room, trades and marketplace activity.
  • Chat: public chat is saved in a log that staff can read. Private messages between friends are stored so they can be delivered, and may be looked at if they're reported or to enforce these Terms.
  • Security information: your IP address is used to limit repeated login tries and sign-ups and to stop abuse. We keep these records only as long as needed for that.
  • Bug reports: what you write, plus the room you were in, the game version, your screen size and your browser type.

How we use it

Only to run the game: logging you in, saving your progress, delivering messages, resetting passwords, keeping players safe, fixing bugs and improving the game. We send email only for account things, like confirming your email or resetting your password.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your information, we don't show ads, and we don't share your information with advertisers.

Cookies and storage

We use one cookie to keep you logged in. The game also saves a few settings in your browser (like your zoom level, sound and which tabs you had open). There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Who else handles it

The game runs on Cloudflare, which hosts the game, stores its data and sends its emails. We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect players or the game.

Your choices

You can change your email or password in MENU > ACCOUNT. To ask for a copy of your information, or to have your account and its information deleted, contact us (see below). Some records, like trades or staff actions, may be kept for a while for safety and fairness.

Players under 18

The game is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we'll remove it.
